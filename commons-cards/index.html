What to try

  • Hover a Commons card. The Commons button in the room header turns white and a glow circles inside its edge in that card’s colour (orange for the HTML page, blue for the Doc, green for the Sheet). The tooltip names the page that will open.
  • Hold the hover. After a moment the panel peeks in from the right edge as a sliver in the same colour. Turn this off with “Peek on hover” to judge the glow on its own.
  • Click a card. The panel slides in and pushes the chat (it never covers it). The card shows Viewing and the Commons button stays pressed in the page’s tint. Hover a different card while one is open to see the glow on top of the pressed state, then click it to switch pages.
  • Press “Replay agent reply”. Matt’s Sutando answers with the cards already in place, marked as being made (dashed preview, grey working line). They fill in when the pages are ready, so there’s one card per page instead of a request card plus a second card.
  • Look for the smaller signals. “Adina is here” on the plan (someone is on the page now), “Updated” on the checklist (edited since it was shared, by whom), and one-line mini cards for Igor’s comment and Adina’s shared update. Edits never post on their own.
  • Switch to Mobile. No hover on touch: tapping a card pushes the page in full screen with Back.
  • Compare card styles. E · Preview is the current pick; C2 · Tinted (full colour with lighter-tinted sections) is here to compare in context.
