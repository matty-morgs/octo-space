Attention signals

Three signals in s.space, each with one job. A slow warm breath means something is waiting on you. A still warm ring marks the answer your Sutando suggests. A quiet grey sweep means an agent is working on it. Everything else stays calm.

1 · Where does the breath live?

Two different messages: “this is waiting on you” is about the whole question, and “your Sutando suggests this answer” is about one action. Four ways to place the breath, all running live on a question with three possible answers. Every card works: click it to mark it seen, or answer it.

A · On the button

Breath on “Take it”. Clear when there’s one action, but with three answers it reads as “press this one” and quietly pushes a choice.

D · Breathing dot, in lists

Recommended for lists

Only the top item breathes, as a small gradient dot that softly swells. The rest keep a still warm dot, and the breathing item’s primary action gets the orange ring. Pairs with C when you open an item.

2 · In review mode: the question breathes

The card around the question breathes while it waits. Click the card and it settles into Seen: the breath fades out and the suggestion ring stays. Then try each answer. Take it (Y) and Decline (N) are decisions, so the breath pours into the button you press and the card folds into a note. Later (L) is a snooze, so the breath just stops.

What happens when you press Take it

1 · Waiting on you

The card breathes. Your Sutando’s suggested answer has the still ring.

2 · The breath pours in

The glow leaves the card and fills the button you pressed. That’s the confirmation: your attention became an action.

3 · It folds into a note

The question becomes a small note in the thread. Rui’s agent hands over the context, with the grey sweep while it works.

4 · It’s yours

The edge turns grey. The note keeps Open and Undo, the item leaves Needs you and shows in Activity › Moving, and review mode moves to the next question.

5 · Agent working: a quiet sweep

A second, separate signal. The breath means something is waiting on you. A grey sweep across text means an agent is working on something right now. It stays neutral, never orange, so it never competes with what needs you, and it only ever runs on live status text. You can also see it in section 2: answer the card with Take it.

6 · Why a dark primary button

Weave 0.11 has since made Primary near-black, so this is now the system. In this direction, colour already carries meaning: agent and room marks are colourful, orange means “waiting on you”, and blue is links and mentions. A near-black primary stays the strongest thing on screen without adding another colour to the mix, and it matches the dark send button.

Click any button to see its focus ring. On blue, the ring is the same colour as the button.

Where blue fails is meaning, not readability. Text on blue passes. The trouble is collisions: buttons, links, mentions and focus all share one blue, and dark mode makes the button identical to a link. If the team wants blue, give links a different colour and keep a 2px focus gap. Either way, point every primary button at one Button/Primary token, so this stays a single change. Note on the orange ring: on white it is 2.0:1, under the 3:1 guide for non-text. It is fine as a supporting cue because the Sutando mark and text carry the meaning. If it needs to pass on its own, the light-mode ring could deepen to #C5793C (3.4:1).

7 · Rules and spec

For engineering. Values match the Figma components “Question card (breathing)” and “Margin light (breathing)”.

Rules

  • 01Breath means “waiting on you”. Never use it for decoration, success, or selection.
  • 02One breathing thing per screen. In lists, only the top waiting item, as a gradient dot, with its primary action ringed.
  • 03It lives on the question (card or margin), never under a button label.
  • 04A suggested answer gets a still 2px warm ring and the Sutando mark. No motion.
  • 05Stop when the item is seen or answered, and after about 3 breaths if ignored.
  • 06Reduced motion: show the Rest state only, with no drift.
  • 07Your own actions, like Reply, never breathe.
  • 08When you decide or send, the breath pours into the button you pressed and the card folds into a note. The note’s gradient edge turns grey once the hand-off is done, so orange still only means waiting on you.
  • 09The pour settles to a colour by answer: full gradient for the suggested yes (Take it), pink for Decline, yellow for Hand to Rui. The note’s edge matches while the agent works, then turns grey.
  • 10Opening something is not answering it. Open stops the breath but keeps the item in Needs you, with a hollow dot, until the work is done. Snooze stops the breath with no pour.
  • 11A grey sweep means an agent is working. Only on live status text, never on headings or on anything waiting on you.
